# Livin Up Productions — Implementation Plan

## Product and implementation
Build a responsive, static five-page marketing site that remakes the supplied homepage, About-page, and Services-page references and adds Portfolio and Contact pages. Use semantic HTML, shared CSS, and vanilla JavaScript; retain the current Python static Preview server on port 3000. Home stays at `/`, About is `/about/`, Services is `/services/`, Portfolio is `/portfolio/`, and Contact is `/contact/`; declare all five in `manus-routes.json`. Keep the shared header, footer, typography, imagery, jewel mark, accessible menu behavior, and contact destinations consistent. No application server, database, authentication, or external business integration is required.

## Design direction
- **Design Movement:** Cinematic East African editorial, with the restrained tactility of analog film and printed cultural journals.
- **Core Principles:** Human-centered storytelling; recognizable photographic moments; confident condensed display type; warm, hand-finished details without ornamental clutter.
- **Color Philosophy:** Soft paper ivory gives the page an approachable, archival base; deep ink and charcoal make the photography immersive; antique brass and burnt umber lend the geometric mark a crafted, ownable quality.
- **Layout Paradigm:** A contained editorial sheet with a white shared masthead, full-bleed film-frame homepage hero, asymmetric About-page editorial spread, paired service cards with strong illustrated panels, a process-and-package pair, and a wide information-rich footer.
- **Signature Elements:** Brass-and-umber faceted jewel mark; perforated film-strip framing and sprocket details; ivory paper grain with pale jewel watermarks and cinematic black overlays.
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Navigation uses a compact ivory capsule for the four primary links, a subtle active-route marker, and a brass “CONTACT US” action linking to `/contact/`; routes remain direct. Each illustrated Services card has a labelled “Learn more” action that opens a native detail dialog; the dialog retains the service-specific email inquiry and a `/contact/` alternative, while package calls to action remain direct email drafts. Hover and focus states feel like a focus pull—clear, quiet, and immediate.
- **Animation:** Brief, restrained image/CTA transitions only; no autoplay or scroll-jacking. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and maintain legible focus-visible states.
- **Typography System:** Barlow Condensed for uppercase titles, navigation, and compact labels; DM Sans for readable supporting copy. Use a clear display/section/body/caption hierarchy.
- **Brand Essence:** Nairobi-based storytellers connecting art, culture, and people through authentic documentary and production work. Personality: cinematic, grounded, generous.
- **Brand Voice:** Human, confident, culturally attentive. Examples: “Global stories. Authentic content.” and “We take our cue from authentic human connection.”
- **Wordmark & Logo:** A custom geometric faceted jewel rendered as an inline vector mark beside a bold two-line LIVIN UP / PRODUCTIONS wordmark; do not use a plain-text substitute for the symbol.
- **Signature Brand Color:** Patinated brass — a warm, aged gold used for jewel facets, the CTA, links, and fine rules.
